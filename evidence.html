Keep the evidence safe

Evidence can help you explain what happened when contacting a platform, bank, or reporting authority. Choose the type of evidence you need to preserve.

Social Media Evidence

Useful when an account has been hacked, impersonated, or used to send suspicious messages.

  • Save your username and profile link.
  • Record the date you noticed the problem.
  • Save screenshots of unfamiliar profile changes.
  • Keep security alerts about unfamiliar logins or password changes.

  • Capture relevant messages with their dates and times.
  • Keep the sender's username or profile link.
  • Preserve messages showing suspicious requests or threats.
  • Do not edit the original screenshots.

  • Save password-change notifications.
  • Keep alerts about new devices or login locations.
  • Save messages from the platform's official support team.

Banking and Wallet Evidence

Useful when money has been transferred without permission or lost through a scam.

  • Save the transaction receipt.
  • Record the transaction ID or reference number.
  • Note the transaction date, time, and amount.
  • Keep available recipient details for the bank or reporting authority.

  • Save messages exchanged with the suspected scammer.
  • Keep relevant phone numbers, usernames, and payment requests.
  • Save complaint reference numbers and responses from your bank.

  • Contact your bank through its official app or phone number.
  • Ask about securing your account or card.
  • Never share your PIN, password, or OTP as evidence.
  • Do not post bank statements or full account details publicly.

Email Evidence

Useful when dealing with phishing emails, suspicious attachments, or unauthorised account access.

  • Save the sender's email address.
  • Record the subject line and date received.
  • Check whether the sender address looks different from the organisation it claims to represent.

  • Preserve the original email where possible.
  • Take screenshots showing the message and sender.
  • Keep suspicious links visible in your evidence without opening them.
  • Do not download or open suspicious attachments.

  • Save unfamiliar login notifications.
  • Keep password-reset and recovery notifications.
  • Record the date and time of suspicious account activity.

Important Things to Remember

  • Keep original evidence whenever possible.
  • Do not delete relevant messages or notifications.
  • Store copies somewhere secure.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs, or OTPs with anyone.
  • Only share evidence with trusted authorities or official support channels.
