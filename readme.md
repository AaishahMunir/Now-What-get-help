# Now What? – Get Help

### A step-by-step guide for when you get hacked or scammed.

## About the Project

Now What? is a website I created to help people figure out what to do when something goes wrong online.

Getting hacked, falling for a scam or receiving online threats can be confusing, especially when you don't know where to start. This website aims to make things a little easier by providing simple instructions and guidance for different situations.

## What You Can Do on the Website

- Choose from four common online security situations.
- Answer a few questions to get guidance related to your situation.
- Follow an interactive checklist and track your progress.
- Search cybersecurity terms and explore different categories.
- Learn how to preserve digital evidence.
- Find information about reporting cybercrime in Pakistan.
- Share feedback through the feedback form.

1. Download the project folder.
2. Open the folder on your computer.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Note

This website was developed as an educational project. The guidance provided is for general awareness and should not replace professional cybersecurity or legal assistance.

The feedback form is a demonstration feature and does not actually send or store submitted responses.
