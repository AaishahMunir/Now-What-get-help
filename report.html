You don't have to deal with this alone

If you've experienced cybercrime, there are official channels where you can seek help and report the incident.

Where can you report cybercrime?

In Pakistan, you can use these official channels to report cybercrime.

If you are in immediate physical danger, move to a safer place and contact local emergency services or a trusted person. In Pakistan, you can call Police Emergency Helpline 15.

1. NCCIA Online Complaint Portal

Submit your cybercrime complaint through the official online portal.

Visit Complaint Portal

2. NCCIA Helpline

Call 1799 for assistance with cybercrime-related complaints.

Never share your passwords, OTPs or banking PINs over the phone.

3. Visit an NCCIA Reporting Centre

Find an official NCCIA office if you need in-person assistance.

Find a Reporting Centre

Before submitting a complaint

Preparing a few details can help you explain what happened clearly.

1. Save relevant screenshots

Keep screenshots of suspicious messages, conversations, emails, profiles and threats. Include dates and usernames whenever possible.

2. Collect account information

Note down relevant usernames, profile links, email addresses and phone numbers connected to the incident.

3. Keep transaction records

If money was involved, save payment receipts, transaction IDs, dates, amounts and relevant bank or wallet details.

4. Write down what happened

Prepare a short timeline explaining what happened, when it happened and how you discovered the incident.

5. Secure your accounts

Change compromised passwords, enable two-factor authentication and contact your bank if financial information was exposed.

6. Keep your information private

Never share passwords, OTPs or private evidence publicly. If you are under 18, involve a trusted adult when seeking help or making a formal complaint.

What happens after reporting?

After submitting a complaint, the relevant authority may take the following steps:

1. Complaint review

The authorities review the information you submitted to understand the nature of the incident.

2. Verification

You may be contacted to provide additional information, documents or verification in person.

3. Further investigation

If the complaint falls within their authority, it may be assessed for further investigation.

4. Follow-up

Keep your complaint reference number and check for updates through the official reporting channel.
